A bill shows the total. Schools need the breakdown.

A monthly electricity bill rarely shows how consumption splits across classrooms, laboratories, offices and air-conditioning. SchoolWatt places selected meter readings on a digital model of the building, so a reading is tied to a real space and can be compared, trended and reported.

SchoolWatt does

  • Measure selected circuits and equipment
  • Map each reading to a BIM space or asset
  • Flag unusual patterns and missing data
  • Produce traceable monthly reports

SchoolWatt does not

  • Cut electricity use automatically
  • Control breakers or protection settings
  • Claim savings before they are measured
  • Replace qualified electrical personnel

From meter to decision in five steps

Each record carries a meter ID, timestamp, value, unit, quality flag and the building space it belongs to.

  1. Meters and sensorsMeasure kWh, kW, voltage, current and power factor at selected points.
  2. IoT gatewaySends readings securely over the school’s approved network.
  3. Data platformValidates, timestamps, stores and flags missing or stale data.
  4. BIM mappingLinks each meter ID to a space or asset in the building model.
  5. DashboardShows trends, zone comparisons, alerts and reports to authorized staff.

Where the project stands

SchoolWatt is currently at provisional Technology Readiness Level 2: the concept is formulated, and no integrated prototype has been tested yet. Each phase must pass its gate before the next begins.

  1. Requirements and feasibilityMonth 1
  2. Concept and detailed designMonths 2–3
  3. Proof of conceptMonths 4–5 · TRL 3
  4. Integrated lab prototypeMonths 6–7 · TRL 4
  5. Relevant-environment testingMonths 8–9 · TRL 5
  6. Controlled school pilotMonths 10–12
  7. Commercial preparationAfter the pilot
